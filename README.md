## 🌟 Key Features & Innovations

- **100% Active 13 Commercial Intelligence Modules**:
  1. **Visão Geral**: Executive master sales overview & real-time revenue trend graph.
  2. **Leads Recebidos**: Total lead ingestion & average CPL metrics.
  3. **Conversões**: Tracked goal conversion events.
  4. **Sales Accepted Leads (SAL)**: Lead acceptance rate & response velocity.
  5. **Sales Qualified Leads (SQL)**: Qualified sales opportunities pipeline.
  6. **Sales Closed Leads (SCL)**: Closed Won contracts & average ticket size.
  7. **Google Ads**: Performance ROAS 7.4x & paid media acquisition.
  8. **Meta Ads**: Facebook/Instagram campaign ROAS 6.2x & CPL tracking.
  9. **LinkedIn Ads**: B2B enterprise lead acquisition.
  10. **Taxa de Conversão**: Overall conversion rate vs benchmark targets (4.85%).
  11. **Blog Posts**: Organic content traffic & inbound lead performance.
  12. **Investimentos**: Paid media budget allocation execution.
  13. **Funil de Vendas**: Full end-to-end visual pipeline stage flow.

- **Floating Glass HUD Navigation & Hover Submenus**:
  - Interactive hover dropdowns for `Dashboards`, `Cubos de dados`, and `Configurações`.
  - Multi-client dropdown switcher (`CHLEBA`, `ADDSUITE`, `PLAENGE`, `MACKENZIE`, `SEB`).

- **Tableau Live Engine Integration**:
  - Full-screen interactive modal viewer connected to `tableau.addintelligence.com.br` with PDF export capability.

- **Luminous Light Mesh Hero Banner**:
  - Soft ambient gradient backdrop matching the official SMARTVISION geometric emblem colors.
